01 Problem

Why this needed building

Ads were bringing visitors to a pricing page, and session recordings showed most of them leaving within seconds: no scroll, no click, no form. Every agency owner I spoke to described the same manual loop: ask for a budget, get on a call, quote something "flexible" so you're never wrong.

68%of pricing-page visitors left without any interaction (session-recording review)
3/3competitor tools hid the result behind a lead form
0/3offered industry-specific templates. Every business started from a blank spreadsheet

"What if the AI gives the wrong price and I look bad?"The one fear every agency owner raised

For the visitor

How might we give a fast, trustworthy price without forcing a call or a long form?

For the business

How might we turn that moment into a real lead, not a fake number typed just to see a price?

02 Key decision

AI talks. Rules price.

The riskiest assumption in a product like this is trusting a language model to come up with a price. Ask it the same thing twice and you can get two different numbers. For a tool whose whole value is trust, that rules it out.

AI

Conversation & understanding

Reads free-text answers, asks natural follow-ups, and drafts a human-sounding WhatsApp handoff.

Rules engine

Price calculation

The owner sets a price for every answer, so the same inputs always give the same range. AI never touches the number.

03 Testing

Two versions, one open question

If you put the price behind a lead form, you get more leads, but plenty of them are fake numbers. If you don't, the business has no reason to use the tool. I designed two approaches and ran a moderated test with 8 participants (past quote-seekers and IT founders).

V1

Single reveal

Answer six questions, enter your name and number, see the full price.

V2

Progressive reveal

After 2–3 questions a wide free range appears, with no form. Only visitors who want an exact number go on to the gate.

The finding worth keepingNeither version won outright. V1 is simpler to explain and faster to ship, while V2 brings in higher-intent leads. So Devora ships both as an account-level setting, and each business can test what converts for its own audience.
04 Solution

Two surfaces, one product

The visitor's chat

One continuous chat instead of a multi-page form. Every question can be answered with one tap, and free text is always available but never the default.

The owner's dashboard

Questions, pricing logic and leads all live in one dashboard behind a single embed script. Onboarding pulls the business name, logo, industry and colour from a URL, so the owner confirms details instead of filling in a form.

05 Reflection

What stuck with me

Good AI design is knowing where AI earns its place. Here that was the conversation, not the number a business stakes its reputation on.

Testing both gate timings instead of picking a winner taught me more than either version would have on its own. Devora is now live as the cost estimator on the Dubai page from my SEO landing page work.
