01 Context

An awkward thing to ask for

In India, most people with a dating or relationship problem don't book a therapist. It feels too formal, too expensive and too public. They ask a friend, or they search. The client wanted a middle ground: a marketplace of relationship coaches and psychologists you can call privately, for a few minutes, and pay by the minute.

That puts three hard problems on one screen:

Unclear need

"I don't know what kind of help I need"

Users can describe their situation ("we keep fighting", "she left me on read") but not which kind of expert fits it.

Money anxiety

A running meter makes people nervous

Per-minute pricing only works if the cost is obvious before the call and there are no surprises during it.

Trust & safety

Strangers, intimate topics, paid calls

Users need to trust the expert, and experts need protection from abuse. Both have to be designed in from the start.

03 Design

Inside the user app

01

Start from the situation

The home screen asks "What are you looking for…" and answers with six photo tiles. Under them, an AI entry point and popular experts give two ways in: talk now, or get a quick bit of help first.

02

Price, rating and availability on every card

Expert cards follow the same order every time: photo, verified badge, specialisation, rating, price per minute, call button. Filters along the top let people narrow by type of help without leaving the list.

03

An AI that helps you text your crush

"Feeling stuck on what to reply?" Users upload up to three chat screenshots, pick a language (Hinglish, English or Hindi) and a tone (funny, romantic or quirky), and get suggested replies.

04

Safety where it's needed, not hidden in settings

In Recent Calls, a swipe on any call shows Report and Block. Settings opens with an account-warnings counter (1/3) above everything else, so users can see where they stand. Language switching sits right under the profile.

04 Supply side

The expert app

A marketplace only works if the experts stay. Their app is about three jobs: accept requests, see earnings and trust the payout. Appointment requests show status (completed or cancelled), price and duration. The wallet shows every credit, and the revenue view splits calls from chats.
