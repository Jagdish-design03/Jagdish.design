My Journey to Design

Based in Dehradun, currently designing at Code Brew Labs, Chandigarh - working with product teams across the UAE and US to ship mobile apps, AI chatbots, AI agents, and dashboards. I sit close to marketing and engineering, not away from them, because good design decisions rarely happen in isolation.

My focus isn't just interfaces - it's root problems. Every project I take on gets pulled apart with research first: where users drop off, why a flow fails, what data actually says versus what the brief assumes. Then I design the fix, not just the screen.

What I Focus On

Product Design

— End-to-end ownership — research, flows, structure, and the decisions that hold a product together.

UX/UI Design

— Interfaces built on heuristic analysis and behavior data, not aesthetic guesswork.

AI-Augmented Workflow

— Figma, Claude, and AI-native tools built into my process daily — for speed without cutting research or reasoning.

Approach

Diagnose

— Behavior tracking (FigJam, Microsoft Clarity, Google Analytics) and heuristic analysis to find where a product is actually failing - not where it's assumed to be failing.

Reframe

— Every problem gets root-caused before it gets designed. I've picked up failing/loss-making products mid-project and traced the actual issue before touching a screen.

Design, faster

— AI tools compress the exploration phase - UX Magic, Claude Code, Claude Cowork - so more time goes into decisions, less into repetition.

Ship & collaborate

— Close handoff with developers and in-house marketing teams - design that survives contact with build.
