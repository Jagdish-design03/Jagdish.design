The challenge

"Mobile app development company in Dubai" is one of the most contested searches in the region. Page one is a wall of agencies saying the same thing.

The pages had to rank, by covering the topic better than competitors, and convert, by earning enough trust for a founder to share their phone number. Most pages in this category manage only one of the two.

What the research showed

Buyers search for cost, compliance and proof

Generic service lists answer none of those questions.

Most visitors never reached the form

Recordings showed people leaving before the contact block at the bottom.

Each market trusts different signals

UAE PASS in Dubai, Nafath and Arabic in Riyadh, HIPAA and SOC 2 in New York.

Key decisions

Lead form in the hero

Headline keyword, three trust chips and a four-field form, side by side. The line under the form answers the two worries people have: "reply within 24 hours" and "NDA available".

Proof in the first two scrolls

Client logos, big numbers and ratings sit right under the hero. Every results card leads with a metric, like "35% fewer missed appointments".

Local proof, not a translated template

The pages share one component system but not their content. Each has its own compliance grid, written for that market's buyers and procurement teams.

What I learned

Good SEO design is mostly good UX: answer the question someone typed, in the order they'd ask it.

Next time I'd set up conversion tracking per section before launch, so the data shows which blocks actually move people to the form.
